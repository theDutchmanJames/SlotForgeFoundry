The Kevan Sideclip: a road connector that slides in from the side of the track and is held by two countersunk wood screws.

## What's new

- **Kevan Sideclip:** a new road connector. A flat clip slides into a pocket from the side of the track, across the joint, and sits flush with the side face.
- **Held by screws:** one countersunk wood screw in each piece cuts its thread into the clip and into a pre-drilled pilot hole beyond it. No keepers needed.
- **Your choice of screws:** screws from the top, from the bottom, or per side of the road. Screw size, pilot hole, countersink and clip size are all adjustable.
- **Automatic screw length:** leave Screw length empty and Foundry picks the longest common length that fits your road height.
- **Track Planner:** joins pieces only when their clips match, and the parts list counts the clips and the screws you need.

## Details

Choose **Kevan Sideclip + two screws** under **Joints → Project clip type**. The clip and screw settings appear below it and belong to the pack's print profile, so every piece in a pack matches.

- The defaults suit 2.5 mm countersunk wood screws: a 2.1 mm pilot hole, a 2.8 mm clearance hole and a 5 mm head at 90°. The clip is 30 × 18 × 3 mm with 6 mm inner corners, with 0.3 mm of play in its pocket.
- Each screw hole keeps at least 4 mm of clip around it. The pocket sits halfway up the road, so screws from the top and from the bottom grip the same: on a 12 mm road a 10 mm screw has 3 mm of thread in the clip and 2.35 mm in the road.
- Clips sit at both road edges of every road joint with clips. Switch either side off for a piece under **Road connections**, where you can also choose each side's screw direction.
- On hollow pieces the pocket and screw holes get solid material around them. Pockets keep clear of guide slots, braid recesses and other connectors, and conflicts are explained with a setting that fixes them.
- Kevan Sideclips join road ends only. Attach and join borders with tabs, and fit the clip before a border on that side, because the border covers the side face.
- The fit samples include the Kevan Sideclip with three amounts of play, and the print kit comes with fitting instructions.

The pack rules box now shows clip types by name.

Pieces that use the draw key, the Carrera clip or tabs build exactly as in v1.1.0.

The [user guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/blob/v1.2.0/docs/GUIDE.md) explains the Kevan Sideclip under **Geometry supported in this release**.

## Verification

781 unit tests passed on Windows, among them real builds of the Kevan Sideclip: its pocket and clip, the screw holes from the top and the bottom, the material around them, mating in the Track Planner, mirrored corners, and every settings check. The browser stages clicked through the shipped app with networking disabled, including choosing and setting up the Kevan Sideclip.

An independent review built joined pieces and confirmed that the clip fits both halves of the pocket with the stated play, that every screw hole keeps solid material around it, and that every refusal names a value that works. Over 220 pieces without the new clip were compared with v1.1.0, mesh for mesh, with no differences.

The macOS and Linux downloads are built and tested by GitHub Actions after this release is published, on each operating system.
