Buildings take a big step: new roofs and shapes, insides, ruins, props, and 71 more standard buildings.

## What's new

- **Standard buildings:** 138 to start from, up from 67: circuit, town and car buildings, stands, towers, canopies, carports, a drive-in screen and a banner bridge.
- **Roofs and shapes:** sawtooth, barrel vault, gambrel, mansard and dome roofs, dormers, chimneys and gutters; octagons, round and curved walls, courtyards, jetties and stepped gables.
- **Openings:** arched and round windows, sills, lintels, shutters, panelled and planked doors, doors that swing on pins, and stone or half-timbered walls.
- **Inside and ruins:** partitions and stairs between floors; leave out the roof or a wall, or break a wall away from the top.
- **Props:** 114 props in groups and kits, from tyre stacks to car lifts, inside a building, around it or on their own.
- **Kits:** look from a side, cut away and measure; a 3MF and footprint sheets in every kit; the Track Planner checks steps, stairs and props stand clear of the track.

## Details

**Roofs.** Besides flat, sloped and two-sided roofs there are sawtooth roofs with north lights, barrel vaults, gambrel and mansard roofs with dormers, hip and cone roofs on shaped buildings, and domes. Chimneys, rooflights, gutters and downpipes can go on any building.

**Shapes.** A shaped building can be an octagon, round, or drawn with curved walls, with storeys of its own, open courtyards (rectangular, eight-sided, round or drawn), upper storeys that jut out over the one below, and a stepped gable at the front.

**Openings and walls.** Windows and doors can be square, arched, pointed or round, with sills, lintels and shutters; a door can be plain, panelled, planked or half-glazed, and on a box building a square door can swing, its leaf a part of its own on pins. Walls can be stone or half-timbered.

**Inside and ruins.** Partitions with a doorway stand on any floor, and stairs climb from one floor to the next through a stairwell. Leave out the roof or any wall to look inside, and break gaps down from the top of a wall for a ruin.

**Props.** 114 props in groups (garage, pit lane, grid, marshals, scrutineering, slot cars, circuit, interiors, architecture, street, shapes, moving parts and machinery), each printing without supports. Put them inside a building, around it or under a canopy, or print a kit of them.

**Seeing a kit.** Look at a building from the front, back, sides or top, cut it away, measure it and hide parts. Every kit has a 3MF and full-size footprint sheets. A fit test prints three grooves to find the joint clearance for your printer.

**Checked again.** A review of the whole Buildings feature found 45 cases where some settings made parts too big for the plate, parts that ran into each other, wrong kit notes or fields that reset while you typed. All are fixed. In the Track Planner a building's door steps, outside stairs, props and stand now count as part of it, so they keep off the track. The Brick hut's windows sit 4 mm lower so its lettering clears the lintel.

The [user guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/blob/v1.8.0/docs/GUIDE.md) explains every setting.

Foundry is not code-signed. On Windows, choose **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** the first time. On Linux, mark the AppImage as executable (`chmod +x`). The SHA256SUMS files list each download's checksum.

Your Library, layouts, buildings and settings carry over from earlier versions.
