A new look for every page, a new track pack made step by step, and borders for a whole pack.

## What's new

- **New track pack in steps:** Basics first, then Road, Slot and braid, Connections, Borders, Lines and kerbs, Road surface and Trackside, each optional.
- **Pack rules in one window:** the same steps for the pack you have, the shared print profile included, applied to every piece at once.
- **Borders for a pack:** one width at the ends of every straight and corner, a wider apex on corners, for all pieces and new ones.
- **Track Set:** pieces in groups, a red Pack rules button, a dot where a piece has its own settings, and Apply to this piece or Apply to Pack Rules.
- **A new look:** Home, Track Planner, Settings and the Library in the same style, with settings in cards and calmer buttons.
- **Split & rebuild** has its own place in STL Edit.

## Details

**New track pack.** The window is larger and shows its steps in a column on the left. Only **Basics** is needed: the name, what the pack starts with, and for an empty pack its road width, lanes and corners. The other steps are optional and hold their settings in folders; each step shows what it holds now, a green dot marks the ones you changed, and **Create track pack** works from any step. A step you leave alone keeps what the chosen pack starts with.

**Pack rules.** Pack rules opens the same window for the pack that is open: Dimensions, Road, Slot and braid, Connections, Borders, Lines and kerbs, Road surface, Trackside and Print segments. The Shared print profile is now part of it. **Apply to all pieces** gives every changed step to every piece; all pieces are checked first, so a setting one piece cannot take leaves the pack as it was.

**Borders for a pack.** **Pack rules → Borders** sets which sides have a border, its width at the ends (the same on every piece, so the borders of two pieces meet), a wider apex for corners, and how the border is fixed. Special pieces you already have keep their own borders.

**Track Set.** The column on the left groups the pack's pieces as straights, corners, drawn pieces and special pieces, and adds a special with one click. A green dot on a piece, and on a section above the model, marks settings of its own (borders, lines, road surface, trackside); that section offers **Use the pack rules**. **Apply to this piece** keeps an edit on the piece; **Apply to Pack Rules** makes it the pack's, for every piece. The settings of each section are grouped in cards.

**Home, Track Planner, Settings and Library.** Home starts with the ways to make a new pack and draws your print plate. The Track Planner groups its pieces, shows the state of the layout beside its name (open ends, Closed loop) and has its section and layout settings in cards. Settings shows one group at a time beside a column that says what each holds. The Library cards keep two buttons and show their other actions as small icons. Red is kept for the main action of each area; the others are a muted red.

**Fixed.** Saving the dimensions under Pack rules no longer drops the pack's lines and kerbs, road texture and trackside settings.

The [user guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/blob/v1.6.0/docs/GUIDE.md) explains every setting.

## Not yet printed

The road surface texture and the sloped pieces from 1.5.3 have still not been printed and tried on a track. The notes of [v1.5.3](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/releases/tag/v1.5.3) list what to check first.

## Verification

930 unit tests passed on Windows, among them tests for pack borders, for a piece with settings of its own and for Pack rules keeping its other settings when its dimensions are saved. The browser stages clicked through the shipped app with networking disabled, a new pack made step by step and a border applied to the pack rules from Track Set among them. The desktop app started and its Settings → Updates were checked.

The macOS and Linux downloads are built and tested by GitHub Actions after this release is published, on each operating system.

## Install

| System | Installer | Portable app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.6.0-Setup-x64.exe` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.6.0-Portable-x64.exe` |
| macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.6.0-mac-universal.dmg` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.6.0-mac-universal.zip` |
| Linux (x64) | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.6.0-linux-amd64.deb` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.6.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage` |
| Linux (ARM) | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.6.0-linux-arm64.deb` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.6.0-linux-arm64.AppImage` |
| Any browser | `SlotForge-Foundry-1.6.0.zip`: extract it and open **SlotForge Foundry.html** | |

Foundry is not code-signed. On Windows, choose **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** the first time. On Linux, mark the AppImage as executable (`chmod +x`). The SHA256SUMS files list each download's checksum.

Your Library, layouts and settings carry over from earlier versions.
