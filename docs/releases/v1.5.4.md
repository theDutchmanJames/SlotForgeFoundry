A fix for sloped pieces: no thin sheet left standing at the sloped end.

## What's new

- **Sloped pieces:** the sloped end is cut cleanly to the joint line; no thin sheet of the old end face sticks out any more.

## Details

**A thin sheet at the sloped end.** In 1.5.3 the sloped end of a sloped piece was cut back to an upright face, so that it meets the tilted straight behind it. On the straights of the starter packs, with their dovetail keys and hollow underside, that cut could leave a paper-thin sheet of the old, leaning end face standing beyond the end, and a few loose bits inside the road. In the Track Planner it showed as a diagonal sheet hanging from the top edge of the joint. The end is now cut in one clean plane, and every sloped piece is one solid body.

Pieces already placed in a layout were made with 1.5.3: place them again, or open the layout once, so they are made anew.

The [user guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/blob/v1.5.4/docs/GUIDE.md) explains every setting.

## Not yet printed

The road surface texture and the sloped pieces from 1.5.3 have still not been printed and tried on a track. The notes of [v1.5.3](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/releases/tag/v1.5.3) list what to check first.

## Verification

917 unit tests passed on Windows, among them one that makes all four sloped forms from the starter pack's straight, with and without the solid base, and finds each one a single clean body ending on its joint lines. It fails on 1.5.3. The browser stages clicked through the shipped app with networking disabled.

The macOS and Linux downloads are built and tested by GitHub Actions after this release is published, on each operating system.
