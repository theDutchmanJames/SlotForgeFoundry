A fix for edits of a pack piece that were lost, with white lines and kerbstones for whole packs and for sections of a layout.

## What's new

- **Edits are kept:** a pack piece no longer comes back as it was before your changes after you open another piece.
- **Markings for a pack:** set white lines and kerbstones once in the Pack rules and apply them to every piece.
- **Markings in a layout:** switch white lines and kerbs on or off for the selected sections in the Track Planner.
- **Green borders:** borders have a greenish tint in the 3D view and on the plan.

## Details

**Edits of a pack piece were lost.** In 1.5.0 and earlier, **Apply changes** saved a piece into its pack, but opening another piece from the rail and coming back showed the piece as it was when the pack was first opened. The next Apply then saved that old state over your edits. White lines and kerbstones were where it showed, but every setting of a piece was affected. A piece is now opened as the pack has it stored.

If a piece lost settings this way, set them again once; they now stay.

**White lines and kerbstones in the Pack rules.** **Pack rules → White lines and kerbstones** has the sides, the sizes and the paint grooves for the pack. **Apply to all pieces** writes them into every piece, and a piece added later starts with them. A single piece can still be changed under **Track Set → Markings**.

**White lines and kerbs in the Track Planner.** Select a section and open **White lines & kerbs** to switch them on or off for the road and for the borders. Ctrl-click or Shift-click selects more sections first, as for the barriers. Line and kerb sizes come from the piece. Markings set in a layout stay when the pack changes.

The [user guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/blob/v1.5.1/docs/GUIDE.md) explains every setting.

## Verification

840 unit tests passed on Windows. The browser stages clicked through the shipped app with networking disabled; one of them now applies an edit, opens another piece of the pack, comes back and finds the edit kept.

The macOS and Linux downloads are built and tested by GitHub Actions after this release is published, on each operating system.

## Install

| System | Installer | Portable app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.5.1-Setup-x64.exe` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.5.1-Portable-x64.exe` |
| macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.5.1-mac-universal.dmg` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.5.1-mac-universal.zip` |
| Linux (x64) | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.5.1-linux-amd64.deb` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.5.1-linux-x86_64.AppImage` |
| Linux (ARM) | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.5.1-linux-arm64.deb` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.5.1-linux-arm64.AppImage` |
| Any browser | `SlotForge-Foundry-1.5.1.zip`: extract it and open **SlotForge Foundry.html** | |

Foundry is not code-signed. On Windows, choose **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** the first time. On Linux, mark the AppImage as executable (`chmod +x`). The SHA256SUMS files list each download's checksum.

Your Library, layouts and settings carry over from earlier versions.
