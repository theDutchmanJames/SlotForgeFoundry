A building editor you can draw in, lettering on any building, finishes per wall, and a new way to find the standard buildings.

## What's new

- **Draw buildings:** drag the walls of a shaped building in or out, pull out a bay, cut a corner at 45°, and join halls and wings, each with its own roof.
- **Windows together:** choose several windows and mirror, space, centre or copy them; every window can have a frame and panes.
- **Lettering:** words on any wall, sign, billboard, gantry or pit fascia, raised or cut in.
- **Finishes:** a texture for each wall, a band of brick or blocks along the foot, a shipping-container shell.
- **Kits:** a full-size glazing template for every window, and a joint clearance to suit your printer.
- **Find a building:** the standard buildings as pictures in groups, with a search, and a new building started from Home.

## Details

**Drawing and editing.** The Buildings page has a **Draw** tab. In **Wall elevation**, drag windows and doors, resize them by a corner, move them with the arrow keys, and undo or redo any edit. Shift-click or Ctrl-click to choose several, then **Mirror across the wall**, **Space evenly**, **Centre on the wall**, **Copy** to another wall or **Remove** them. Tick **Frame** for a frame round an opening, with up to six panes across and up. In **Footprint**, an L, U or T building can be drawn wall by wall: drag a wall in or out, **Pull out a bay** or **Push in a recess**, or **Cut the corner at 45°**. Walls run square or at 45°, and a change that leaves no room inside is refused with the reason. **Connected halls and wings** joins up to eight sections, each with its own size, height and flat, lean-to, gable or hip roof.

**Lettering.** **+ Lettering** puts words on a wall of any storey, raised 0.6 mm or **Cut in, not raised**. The texture keeps a smooth patch behind them, and they keep clear of windows, doors, signs and each other. A sign along the wall, a billboard or hoarding, a start gantry's board, a flag post's board and a pit building's name board take text too, raised or cut. A roof billboard prints face down, so its text is carved in V-grooves that print without supports. The letters are block capitals, figures and a few marks, at least 0.8 mm wide. The standard buildings carry plain names, never brands: RACE CONTROL, PRESS, POST 1 to 6, START and FINISH, among others.

**Finishes.** **A wall of its own** gives any wall its own texture, and **Lower walls** lays a band of another along the foot of a storey. Connected and shaped buildings now take textures and framed windows too. **Container shell** adds corner posts, rails, a ribbed roof and cargo doors to one storey; the Container café and Container office use it.

**Standard buildings.** Seven presets are reworked in more detail: VIP and hospitality, timing box, marshal hut, classic garage, container office and refreshment kiosk, and the new **Race headquarters**. The starting shapes (L, U, T and Connected halls and wings) now begin with brick or planks, a band at the foot and framed windows.

**Kits.** **Export kit** adds `glazing.svg` when a building has windows: each clear sheet full size and named, 2 mm larger than its window all round, a trapezoid behind a leaning wall. Print it at 100 % and cut clear plastic 0.3 to 0.5 mm thick. **Joint clearance** (0.1 to 0.5 mm) sets how loose the walls sit in their grooves.

**The Buildings page and Home.** On the left, the building's parts come first, then your saved buildings, then the standard buildings as pictures in seven groups that fold, with **Find a building**. On the right, every settings card folds from its title; **Fold all** and **Open all** do them all, and the page remembers what is folded. Home has **Start a new building**: a standard building from its picture, a plain garage, a shape to draw or halls and wings, with your saved buildings beside it.

**Fixed.** A solid gantry beam's pin sockets printed over air: the pin and its socket now point at 45°. Switching a lettered start gantry to a footbridge no longer fails.

The [user guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/blob/v1.7.0/docs/GUIDE.md) explains every setting.

## Not yet printed

None of the new buildings, lettering, glazing templates or clearances have been printed yet: every part is checked in software to be one piece, fit the plate, print without supports and stay clear of the others. Print a small sign with lettering first to see how 0.8 mm strokes come out on your printer. The road surface texture and sloped pieces from 1.5.3 are still untested on a track as well.

## Verification

1148 unit tests passed on Windows, with every kit of the new features held to whole pieces, nothing printed over air and no part inside another. Ten browser tests clicked through the Buildings page and Home of the shipped app with networking disabled, and the 69 checks of the main browser suite passed. The Windows desktop app started twice from its build and kept its Library and settings in the portable data folder.

The macOS and Linux downloads are built and tested by GitHub Actions after this release is published, on each operating system.

## Install

| System | Installer | Portable app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.7.0-Setup-x64.exe` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.7.0-Portable-x64.exe` |
| macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon) | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.7.0-mac-universal.dmg` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.7.0-mac-universal.zip` |
| Linux (x64) | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.7.0-linux-amd64.deb` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.7.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage` |
| Linux (ARM) | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.7.0-linux-arm64.deb` | `SlotForgeFoundry-1.7.0-linux-arm64.AppImage` |
| Any browser | `SlotForge-Foundry-1.7.0.zip`: extract it and open **SlotForge Foundry.html** | |

Foundry is not code-signed. On Windows, choose **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** the first time. On Linux, mark the AppImage as executable (`chmod +x`). The SHA256SUMS files list each download's checksum.

Your Library, layouts, buildings and settings carry over from earlier versions.
