Trackside accessories: barriers, advertising boards with catch fences and guard rails that plug into the edge of your track, and a Track Planner that cuts a layout for your print bed when you ask.

## What's new

- **Trackside:** a new section in Track Set adds accessories beside a piece, on tabs in sockets in its edge.
- **Concrete barriers:** your own height and face angle, with a sloped or a sloped and rounded end.
- **Advertising boards and fences:** a flat wall for a paper sign, with a catch fence that plugs into its top.
- **Guard rails:** single or double rails on posts, with every size a setting.
- **Whole packs and layouts:** set trackside once in the Pack rules, or per section in the Track Planner.
- **Segment for printing:** the Track Planner cuts a layout only when you ask, after checking your print bed.

## Details

**Trackside.** Open a piece in Track Set and choose **Trackside → Barrier side**: outside, inside or both. Each accessory stands on the table beside a border segment, or beside the road on a side without a border, and plugs into sockets in that edge with the tab size and clearance set under Joints.

- **Concrete barrier:** the foot is as high as the track and the sloped face rises above the road. Set the **Height above the road** (5 to 100 mm), the **Face angle** (0° to 45°), one or two tabs, and plain, sloped or sloped and rounded ends.
- **Advertising board:** a flat wall with its own thickness and a rounded top corner at an end. Print a sign on paper and glue it to the face.
- **Catch fence:** switch on **Fence on the board**. Each board carries diamond mesh and cables between two square posts that drop into sockets in its top. Set the fence height, post size, cable spacing and mesh size.
- **Guard rails:** a single rail hangs under its top with open space below; a double rail fills from the top to just above the road. Each piece has a post near its finish end, and its rail comes from the post of the piece before it. Two rail ends meet on a post, pinched under a T-shaped peg. Switch on a start post for the first piece of a row.
- **Each side its own:** with barriers on both sides, **Separate settings for the inside** gives the inside its own shape and sizes.
- **Barriers per segment** cuts a barrier into shorter ones, or adds posts to a guard rail.

**Printing.** A straight barrier and a straight board export on their flat back with the tabs up. A fence exports flat in one piece. A rail post exports on its side and a rail exports flat and straight. On a bend, the fence and the rail are still printed flat and bend to follow the board or the posts; a curved barrier or board prints standing and needs support under each tab. **Export STLs → Barriers and fences only** downloads these parts on their own.

**For a whole pack.** **Pack rules → Trackside accessories** has the same settings for the pack. **Apply to all pieces** sets every piece the same way, and a piece added later starts with them. A single piece can still be changed afterwards.

**In the Track Planner.**

- Select a section and open **Barriers** to set the outside and the inside for that section only. Ctrl-click or Shift-click selects more sections.
- A piece is now placed whole. **Segment for printing** asks whether the width, depth and margin are those of your print bed, then cuts every piece that does not fit into the fewest equal segments that do.
- **Export waits for that check:** a layout gives its print kit only after it has been segmented for the print bed that is set.

**Packs start whole.** Every piece of a new pack starts in one road segment; the corners of the starter packs are no longer pre-cut for a 200 mm plate. For a pack made earlier, **Pack rules → Reset every piece to one segment** makes its pieces whole again.

Projects, packs and layouts saved in earlier versions open unchanged, without trackside accessories.

The [user guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/blob/v1.5.0/docs/GUIDE.md) explains every setting.

## Verification

839 unit tests passed on Windows, among them real builds of every trackside shape measured at the sizes that were set, on straights, bends, tapered borders and all special pieces. The browser stages clicked through the shipped app with networking disabled, including an export that waits for the print bed check.

No trackside part has been test-printed. The play of a fence post in its socket, the pinch of a guard rail under its T, and how far a flat fence or rail bends onto a curve are estimates until a first print; the pinch and the sizes are settings for that reason.

The macOS and Linux downloads are built and tested by GitHub Actions after this release is published, on each operating system.

Foundry is not code-signed. On Windows, choose **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** the first time. On Linux, mark the AppImage as executable (`chmod +x`). The SHA256SUMS files list each download's checksum.

Your Library, layouts and settings carry over from earlier versions.
