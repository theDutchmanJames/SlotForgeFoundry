Off-centre guide slots: put the slot of a straight or curve anywhere across the road, and let it change sides along a straight.

## What's new

- **Off-centre slots:** set each slot's distance from the left or right edge (outer or inner on a curve), separately for end A and end B.
- **Smooth lane changes:** a straight whose ends differ runs its slot in a smooth S that meets both joints square.
- **Several lanes:** move all lanes together, or set each lane on its own to change the lane spacing along a piece.
- **Connectors that still fit:** joints keep clear of the slots, ends with the same slot positions always fit, and conflicts are explained.
- **Track Planner:** joins pieces only where the slots line up, and says which piece to make when they do not.

## Details

Choose **Slot position → Off-centre** in **Add track piece**, or under **Shape → Lane layout** in Track Set. Distances are measured from the edge you pick, looking from end A to end B, and settle on half millimetres. The panel shows the room on the road and notes on the tightest bend, the slot angle, slots that come close and car bodies near an edge.

- Left-hand corners in the Track Planner are mirrored prints, so their slot swaps sides. Make a second corner measured from the other edge for left-hand turns.
- To bring the slot back, make a second straight with end A and end B swapped. With tab joints a piece fits one way round only.
- Special pieces, freeform paths and imported drawings keep their own slots. Squeeze / Spread and off-centre slots cannot be combined on one piece.
- Piece names include the slot position, for example **Straight · 300 mm · slot 35-145 from left**.
- When a pack's width, lane count or spacing changes, pieces keep their typed distances where they still fit, and the pack rules box says before you apply which pieces will be centred.

Pieces with a centred slot build exactly as in v1.0.0.

The [user guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/blob/v1.1.0/docs/GUIDE.md) explains slot positions under **Off-centre slots**.

## Verification

764 unit tests passed on Windows, among them real builds of every slot position form, connector mating and mirroring, Track Planner joins and refusals, and pack rule changes. 61 browser stages clicked through the shipped app with networking disabled, including creating, editing, refusing and planning off-centre pieces with one and two lanes.

Independent reviews compared over 2,000 centred pieces and over 400 single-lane off-centre pieces with the previous build, mesh for mesh, with no differences. Over 2,400 connector checks confirmed that ends with the same slot positions mate and that mirrored corners match.

The macOS and Linux downloads are built and tested by GitHub Actions after this release is published, on each operating system.
