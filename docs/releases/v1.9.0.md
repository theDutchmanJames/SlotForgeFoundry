Buildings in colour, floors with textures, and a Rally group with pieces to drive through.

## What's new

- **Colours:** see buildings in their materials or a colour set of your own, kept in the Library, the Track Planner, the kit's 3MF and its paint list.
- **Floor textures:** square tiles, floorboards, flagstones, concrete slabs or brick paving on the floors of boxes, shapes, halls and wings.
- **Rally group:** a timing caravan, a dry-stone wall with a gate and a forest hunting tower, beside the start ramp and service tent.
- **Drive through:** an arch house over the road, a tunnel portal, a rock gallery and a level crossing that the track runs through or past.
- **Draw a shape:** on Home, it now opens a plain shape ready to draw.
- **Halls and wings:** walls now meet face to face where sections join, with no gap at the corner.

## Details

**Colours.** The Colours button shows a building in its materials (brick, stone, timber, roof tiles and so on, each following its texture) or in a set of your own for walls, roof, band, floors, frames and doors. Standard buildings start in colours of their own, their pictures too. The colours are kept with a building in the Library, shown in the Track Planner, written into the kit's 3MF and listed in its paint notes. The lower band can have a colour of its own.

**Floor textures.** A box, a shaped building, and the halls and wings of a connected building can have tiles, floorboards, flagstones, concrete slabs or brick paving cut into their floors, clear of the walls. Each texture has its own colour.

**Rally.** A new **Rally** group in Start from holds seven pieces of their own, each with its own card. The timing caravan has a serving hatch, TIMING lettering, a drawbar and an aerial. The dry-stone wall is laid in rough courses, with a five-bar gate between two gateposts. The hunting tower is a planked cabin on four braced, spreading legs, with a ladder.

**Drive through.** These four span the track, 220 mm clear to start with:
- The arch house has stone piers, shuttered windows on one to three storeys and a balcony over the arch.
- The tunnel portal has a stone headwall with voussoirs and a keystone, a lined tunnel to build a hillside over, and a small house against its side.
- The rock gallery has a rough rock face on one side and arched pillars open to the valley on the other.
- The level crossing brings its rails up to the borders on each side, so the slots stay clear. It has cross signs, half barriers with their booms raised and a keeper's cottage.

The [user guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeFoundry/blob/v1.9.0/docs/GUIDE.md) explains every setting.

## Not yet printed

The new rally pieces and floor textures have been checked in software only. In each, every part is one piece, fits on the plate, prints without supports and is clear of the other parts. Print the Fit test first, then a small piece, before a large one.

## Verification

1400 unit tests passed on Windows. They include all 145 standard buildings, each built and checked part by part, and a check that the road under each drive-through piece is clear the whole way through. The Buildings page, Home and rally browser tests and the main browser suite passed in the shipped app with networking disabled.

GitHub Actions builds and tests the macOS and Linux downloads on each system after this release is published.

Foundry is not code-signed. On Windows, choose **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** the first time. On Linux, mark the AppImage as executable (`chmod +x`). The SHA256SUMS files list each download's checksum.

Your Library, layouts, buildings and settings carry over from earlier versions.
